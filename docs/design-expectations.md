Version 1.0

# Brand Vision

Atelier is an AI-native shopping experience that combines the elegance of editorial retail with the intelligence of a personal stylist.

Instead of browsing endless catalogs, customers discover products through conversation, inspiration, and curated recommendations.

The experience feels less like a store and more like a beautifully merchandised flagship.

---

# Brand Positioning

### Traditional E-Commerce

Search → Filters → Product Grid → Checkout

### Atelier

Conversation → Curation → Discovery → Styling → Checkout

The AI is not the product.

The shopping experience is the product.

# Design Principles

## 1. Editorial First

Every screen should feel like a magazine spread before it feels like software.

Large photography.

Generous whitespace.

Strong typography hierarchy.

Intentional pacing.

---

## 2. Products Are the Hero

Avoid excessive UI chrome.

Avoid cards everywhere.

Avoid dashboard aesthetics.

Products and imagery should dominate the experience.

---

## 3. AI Is Invisible

The customer should feel assisted.

Never overwhelmed by AI terminology.

Avoid:

* Agents
* Copilots
* Assistants
* AI-generated labels

Prefer:

* Curated for you
* Recommended for your style
* Inspired by your closet
* Selected for this occasion

---

## 4. Luxury Through Restraint

Premium brands use less.

Fewer colors.

Fewer borders.

Fewer visual effects.

More breathing room.

# Homepage Structure

## Transparent Header

Logo centered.

Minimal navigation.

Profile icon top-right.

No heavy navbar.

Header overlays hero imagery.

---

## Hero Section

Full-screen lifestyle photography.

Large editorial headline.

Example:

"Designed Around Your Style."

Subheading:

Tell us what you're looking for and discover pieces curated specifically for you.

Single conversational search field.

---

## AI Search Experience

Instead of:

Search products...

Use:

"What are you shopping for today?"

Examples beneath:

* Summer wedding in Italy
* Mid-century living room refresh
* Capsule wardrobe for work
* Gifts for a design lover

---

## Discovery Collections

Large editorial tiles.

Not product cards.

Examples:

Spring Linen Edit

Weekend Escape

Quiet Luxury

Earth Tones

The Modern Home

---

## Personalized Feed

# Motion Design

Slow.

Elegant.

Confident.

Avoid:

* Bouncy animations
* Gamification
* Flashy effects

Use:

* Fade transitions
* Parallax imagery
* Gentle reveals
* Smooth scrolling

---

# Photography Style

Natural light.

Editorial composition.

Luxury lifestyle.

Textured environments.

Neutral palettes.

Premium but attainable.

Never use:

* Stock-photo startup imagery
* Futuristic AI visuals
* Robots
* Abstract technology graphics

"I've entered a beautifully merchandised flagship store that somehow already understands my taste."

Not:
