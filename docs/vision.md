# What is the product?
## An AI-native fashion shopping experience that combines:
- AI personal Styling
- Collaborative shopping with friends
- Editorial fashion discovery
- AI Assisted outfit building
- My closet with pieces previously purchased via platform
- Style DNA with personalized fashion style report

The Goal is to replace search and filter shopping into a more intent-driven shopping experience.

# Who is it for?
## Target User

25-35 year old professionals.

People who:

- like Zara
- like Mango
- use Pinterest
- enjoy fashion inspiration
- feel overwhelmed by large catalogs
- who want to reduce impulse buying
- ask friends for opinions before purchasing

# Core User Journey 

- User Arrives - step by step onboarding process to create account
- Lands in "Concierge Tab" after sign-in/sign-up 
    - Describes shopping intent 
    - series of AI driven questions 
- AI creates outfit directions, moodboard etc. in "Workspace tab"
- User explores and interacts with AI to refine and talk about directions and moodbaord 
- Shows pieces and piece info from catalog to users in workspace tab - "Hanger space"
- user can create various "hangers" of outfits they can make hangers public or private
- Shop woth friends allows friedn to react and chat about style and Hangers in real-time 
- AI Uses info to tailor future iteration of styles and pieces
- AI summaries feedback and attaches reports to hangers.
- User can save and purchases pieces. Saved, carted and purchased pieces can be found in the closet tab.
- My Style DNA adjusts as user interacts more and more. 

# Project principles
1. Conversation before search

2. Stories before products

3. AI feels like a stylist

4. Friends are part of shopping

5. Luxury fashion aesthetic

6. Discovery over filtering
